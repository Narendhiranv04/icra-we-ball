## Provenance

Zero FM calls.  Same 96 archived raw semantic responses as the baseline, replayed
through the deterministic pipeline at the commit recorded below.  Four workshop
trials segfaulted under five-way parallel detector loading on this GPU and were
re-run serially; the packaged rows contain no harness failures.

Two kitchen trials (K1/trial_03, K3/trial_02) hit the per-trial 1500s timeout in
grounding.  Both compile a graph with roles and no operation group, so both were
graph-compilation failures regardless; the cost is joint-assignment enumeration
over a provisional role type and a four-instance utensil role.

`outcome_category` changed meaning between the two runs and the delta table says
so: it is now first-cause attribution, so a contract whose required operations
the runtime could not represent is reported as GRAPH_COMPILATION_FAILURE even
though the pipeline ran on to grounding for diagnostics.  The stage columns
(`graph_compiled` = a graph with roles compiled, `grounding_reached`, `astar_reached`)
are measured identically in both runs and are directly comparable.
