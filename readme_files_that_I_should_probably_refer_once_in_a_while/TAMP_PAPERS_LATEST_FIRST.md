# Task and Motion Planning (TAMP): strict literature corpus

**Coverage cutoff:** 2026-07-14  
**Order:** reverse chronological by first public appearance (arXiv v1 when available; otherwise the earliest located proceedings/journal version).  
**Strict/core corpus:** 216 deduplicated intellectual works.  
**Separate, non-counted foundations:** 9 precursor works.  
**Companion analysis:** [TAMP_NOVELTIES_LIMITATIONS_AND_IDEAS.md](./TAMP_NOVELTIES_LIMITATIONS_AND_IDEAS.md)

## Scope and inclusion rule

This is a TAMP-exclusive corpus. A work is included only when its central method jointly reasons about:

1. a discrete, symbolic, temporal, mode, task-allocation, or skill-sequencing decision; and
2. continuous geometric, kinematic, dynamic, trajectory, contact, controller, or motion-feasibility variables.

TAMP surveys, theory, benchmark papers, and execution/recovery extensions are included. Generic task planning, generic motion planning, one-step grasping, embodied-language planning without continuous feasibility reasoning, and papers that only cite TAMP as motivation are excluded. Early papers are included only when they are genuine intellectual precursors that couple action/mode choices to geometric motion.

Conference papers and their substantially overlapping journal extensions are represented once as one intellectual work; the version lineage is noted in the entry. This prevents an inflated count. The corpus was assembled by backward chaining from the major TAMP surveys, forward searches through arXiv and venue proceedings, author/project bibliographies, and exact-title/phrase variants (for example, *task and motion planning*, *task-and-motion planning*, *combined task and motion planning*, *integrated symbolic and geometric planning*, *logic-geometric programming*, and *bilevel planning*).

## Completeness claim and reproducible search boundary

“All papers” cannot be proved literally: TAMP has no controlled index term, older workshop papers are unevenly indexed, and neighboring communities use different names for the same coupling. This file is therefore a **systematic, best-effort census to the stated cutoff**, not a claim that an undiscoverable paper cannot exist.

Coverage is systematic rather than provably exhaustive: it includes works discoverable by the stated searches through 14 July 2026 that satisfy the operational TAMP definition, while acknowledging that unindexed, non-English, workshop-only, thesis, or differently titled works may have been missed.

The search screened all 263 arXiv records returned by the exact phrase *task and motion planning* at the cutoff, then expanded the candidate pool by:

- backward and forward chaining from the [Annual Review synthesis](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-control-091420-084139), the [optimization-based survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02817), the [ACM general survey](https://doi.org/10.1145/3583136), and the [multi-mobile-robot survey](https://doi.org/10.1145/3564696);
- phrase variants and adjacent historical labels in arXiv, Crossref, DBLP-style venue records, proceedings, publisher pages, and author bibliographies;
- DOI/title/author-lineage deduplication, followed by an abstract/method-level test against the two-part inclusion rule above; and
- a final publisher-first audit for papers that were public but absent from arXiv.

The strict count excludes the separately labelled precursor and exclusion-audit sections. Survey papers are counted because the user requested literature-review coverage; substantially overlapping venue extensions are not double-counted.

## Legend

- **Algorithm:** a general TAMP formulation or solver.
- **Learning:** learning is inside or directly guides the TAMP loop.
- **Uncertainty/execution:** belief, risk, online execution, monitoring, recovery, or partial grounding.
- **Multi-agent/temporal:** allocation, synchronization, scheduling, or concurrent motion.
- **Survey/benchmark/theory:** review, benchmark, representation, completeness, bounds, or framework analysis.
- **Application:** an application-centered TAMP contribution with a reusable integration idea.

## Exclusion audit — representative false positives

These records were screened but are **not counted**. Recording them makes the operational boundary inspectable and prevents title-only search from silently broadening the review.

- **[Hierarchical Prompting with Dual LLM Modules for Robotic Task and Motion Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08330):** the second LLM proposes 3-D spatial placements, but motion execution is a stub; there is no continuous motion-feasibility/refinement layer.
- **[H-WM: Robotic Task and Motion Planning Guided by Hierarchical World Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11291):** a logical/visual world model guides a VLA policy, without an explicit continuous feasibility solver in the planning loop.
- **[Optimal Task and Motion Planning for Autonomous Systems Using Petri Nets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12503):** despite the title, the method solves discrete unit-cost region routing through Petri-net reachability, not continuous geometry or trajectory planning.
- **[GaP: A Graph-as-Policy Multi-Agent Self-Learning Harness for Variational Automation Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05369):** TAMP motivates the system, but the contribution generates and improves computation-graph policies rather than jointly searching task and continuous motion variables.
- **[Learning Motion Feasibility from Point Clouds in Cluttered Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26700):** learns a motion-feasibility component but does not perform task or skill-sequence search.
- **[Bounding Boxes as Goals: Language-Conditioned Grasping via Neuro-Symbolic Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12910):** focuses on a language-conditioned one-step grasp goal, not long-horizon discrete/continuous planning.
- **[Learning-based Directed Graph Abstraction of Combinatorial Spaces for Order-Preserving Search in Mixed-Combinatorial Nonlinear Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01425):** is a generic mixed-combinatorial optimization method; TAMP is an application class rather than the paper's planning formulation.
- **[Verified Task-Space Motion Planning Under Joint-Space Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22991):** verifies continuous motion in task space but contains no task-level action or mode search.
- **[Navigating the Clutter: Waypoint-Based Bi-Level Planning for Multi-Robot Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21138):** optimizes ordered waypoints and paths but does not introduce a symbolic/temporal task layer.
- **Generic embodied-language systems** such as direct VLA policies, code-generation policies, and LLM-only task planners are excluded unless the paper explicitly closes the loop with continuous geometric, kinematic, dynamic, or contact feasibility.
